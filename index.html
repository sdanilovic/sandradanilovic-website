Academic and Creative Portfolio

Sandra Danilovic, PhD

Associate Professor, Game Design & Development, Faculty of Human and Social Sciences, and Affiliate Graduate Faculty, Community Psychology, Wilfrid Laurier University

About

Computer game design researcher, multimedia artist, and filmmaker.

Research areas

Game design for mental health and community health; autobiographical games about illness and disability; game jams for research; inclusive design.

Recent work

  • Game Design TheraPoetics: Making Games with Care (forthcoming 2027), University of California Health Humanities Press.
  • Recovery Game Café: games made by adults with lived experience of opioid addiction in SSHRC-funded game jams.
  • Playful resilience (2024), Honourable Mention, ACM CHI Play.
